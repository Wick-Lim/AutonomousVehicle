\begin{abstract}
Autoregressive vision-language-action (VLA) models \cite{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla,liu2023visual} decode tokens sequentially ($>300$\,ms/frame), exceeding the $50$\,ms budget of $20$\,Hz vehicle control \cite{dosovitskiy2017carla,li2022metadrive}. We implement a single-pass closed-loop driving stack coupling frozen DINOv2 (\texttt{facebook/dinov2-base}) \cite{oquab2024dinov2,caron2021emerging,dosovitskiy2021image} with frozen Laya (\texttt{convaiinnovations/laya}) \cite{laya2024nonautoregressive,warner2024modernbert,devlin2019bert} via a $4 \times 9$ spatial pooling projector ($1.85$M parameters). Cropped $196 \times 392$ RGB frames map to 36 spatial tokens fused with telemetry JSON embeddings; one forward pass outputs maneuver (\texttt{choice}), speed tier (\texttt{score}), and emergency brake (\texttt{noul}) logits in $31.67$\,ms ($31.6$\,FPS). Post-hoc temperature scaling \cite{guo2017calibration} yields $0.0000$ ECE. Lateral waypoint step jumps ($3.5$\,m) and longitudinal speed-bucket hunting in CARLA 0.9.16 (\texttt{Town10HD\_Opt}) \cite{dupuis2010opendrive,hart1968formal} are eliminated using a 14-point ($28$\,m) quintic S-curve spline $s(\tau) = 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5$ \cite{flash1985coordination,werling2010optimal}, decoupled chord-angle tracking \cite{thrun2006stanley,hoffmann2007autonomous,coulter1992implementation,paden2016survey}, linear steering ramps, and constant-time-headway ACC ($d^* = 16.0$\,m, $k_g = 0.85\,\text{s}^{-1}$) \cite{ioannou1993autonomous,rajamani2001experimental,treiber2000congested}. Across $4,153$ MetaDrive 0.4.3 frames and 5 urban FSD scenarios in CARLA 0.9.16, route completion and crash-free rates are $100.0\%$.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Problem Formulation}
Autoregressive decoders evaluate $P(y_{1:K} \mid I_t, \mathcal{T}_t) = \prod_{k=1}^{K} P(y_k \mid y_{<k}, I_t, \mathcal{T}_t)$ over $K \approx 15$ serial steps \cite{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla,liu2023visual}, whereas planning-oriented and sensor-fusion stacks \cite{hu2023planning,jiang2023vad,li2022bevformer,codevilla2018end,chen2020learning,prakash2021multi,chitta2022transfuser,jaeger2023hidden} require $\Delta t \le 50$\,ms. In our formulation, control at step $t$ requires three discrete outputs read in a single bidirectional pass \cite{devlin2019bert,warner2024modernbert,laya2024nonautoregressive}:
\begin{align}
    m_t &\in \{\texttt{keep\_lane}, \texttt{change\_left}, \nonumber\\
        &\quad\; \texttt{change\_right}, \texttt{follow\_lead}\}, \\
    s_t &\in \{0, 1, 2, 3\} \mapsto \{0, 20, 35, 50\}\,\text{km/h}, \\
    p_{\text{AEB}} &\in [0, 1].
\end{align}

\section{Architecture and Implementation}
\label{sec:arch}
Camera frames are cropped ($\texttt{crop\_top}=0.25$, $\texttt{crop\_bottom}=0.10$) and resized to $196 \times 392$. Frozen DINOv2 ViT-B/14 ($p=14$) \cite{oquab2024dinov2,caron2021emerging,dosovitskiy2021image} extracts $F_{\text{patch}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 392 \times 768}$ ($14 \times 28$ grid). \texttt{SpatialProjector} (\texttt{src/models/spatial\_projector.py}, trained with AdamW \cite{loshchilov2019decoupled}) pools $14 \times 28 \to 4 \times 9$ (36 tokens) and projects $768 \to 1024$:
\begin{equation}
    G = \text{AdaptiveAvgPool2d}_{(4,9)}(\tilde{F}) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 768 \times 4 \times 9},
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
    V_{\text{spatial}} = \text{MLP}\big(\text{Flatten}(G) + E_{\text{pos}}\big) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 36 \times 1024}.
\end{equation}
\begin{scriptsize}
\begin{verbatim}
# src/models/spatial_projector.py
grid = feat_2d.permute(0, 3, 1, 2)
pooled = self.pool(grid).flatten(2).transpose(1, 2)
tokens = self.mlp(pooled + self.pos_embed)
\end{verbatim}
\end{scriptsize}
Multimodal sequence $Z = [e_{\text{[CLS]}}; V_{\text{spatial}}; E_{\text{telemetry}}; e_{\text{choice}}; e_{\text{score}}; e_{\text{noul}}]$ is evaluated in one forward pass $H = f_{\text{Laya}}(Z)$ \cite{laya2024nonautoregressive,warner2024modernbert}:
\begin{align}
    z^{(m)} &= W_m H_{:, \text{idx}_{\text{choice}}}, \quad
    z^{(s)} = W_s H_{:, \text{idx}_{\text{score}}}, \nonumber\\
    z^{(b)} &= W_b H_{:, \text{idx}_{\text{noul}}}.
\end{align}

\section{Calibrated Safety Shield, Quintic Spline, and ACC}
\label{sec:control}
Temperature scaling \cite{guo2017calibration} calibrates probabilities $\hat{p}^{(m)} = \text{Softmax}(z^{(m)}/T_m)$ and $\hat{p}_{\text{AEB}} = \sigma((z^{(b)}_1 - z^{(b)}_0)/T_b)$. Rule~1 forces $u_{\text{tb}} = -1.0$ when $\hat{p}_{\text{AEB}} \ge 0.70$, $\texttt{traffic\_light}=\texttt{red}$, or pedestrian distance $d \le 16.0$\,m. Rule~2 reverts lane changes to \texttt{keep\_lane} if $\max_c \hat{p}^{(m)}_c < 0.80$ or if OpenDRIVE same-direction lane check $\texttt{adj\_wp.lane\_id} \times \texttt{wp.lane\_id} > 0$ fails \cite{dupuis2010opendrive}.

For lane transitions over $N_b = 14$ waypoints ($28.0$\,m, $\tau_k = k/(N_b - 1)$), 3D coordinates are blended via minimum-jerk quintic polynomial \cite{flash1985coordination,werling2010optimal}:
\begin{align}
    s(\tau) &= 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5, \\
    P^{\text{blend}}_k &= \big(1 - s(\tau_k)\big) P^{\text{orig}}_k + s(\tau_k) P^{\text{adj}}_k.
\end{align}
\begin{scriptsize}
\begin{verbatim}
# src/env/carla_wrapper.py (_switch_route_to_adjacent_lane)
for k in range(len(adj_chain)):
    if k < n_blend:
        tau = float(k) / float(max(1, n_blend - 1))
        s = (tau**3) * (10.0 + tau * (-15.0 + 6.0 * tau))
        bx = (1.0 - s) * float(ol.x) + s * float(al.x)
        by = (1.0 - s) * float(ol.y) + s * float(al.y)
\end{verbatim}
\end{scriptsize}
Continuous segment projection onto $\overline{P_i P_{i+1}}$ gives foot point $P_{\text{proj}}$ and arc-length lookahead $P_{\text{look}}$ at $L_{\text{look}} = \text{clip}(7.5 + 0.18 v_{\text{kmh}}, 7.5, 13.5)$\,m \cite{thrun2006stanley,hoffmann2007autonomous,coulter1992implementation,paden2016survey}. Defining chord angle $\psi_{\text{ref}} = \text{atan2}(y_{\text{look}} - y_{\text{proj}}, x_{\text{look}} - x_{\text{proj}})$ decouples cross-track offset $e_y$ and heading error $e_\psi$:
\begin{align}
    e_y &= (x - x_{\text{proj}})\sin\psi_{\text{proj}} - (y - y_{\text{proj}})\cos\psi_{\text{proj}}, \\
    e_\psi &= -\text{atan2}\big(\sin(\psi_{\text{ego}} - \psi_{\text{ref}}), \cos(\psi_{\text{ego}} - \psi_{\text{ref}})\big), \\
    \Delta \delta_{\max} &= \frac{1.1\Delta t}{1 + 0.012 v_{\text{kmh}}}, \\
    \delta_t &= \delta_{t-1} + \text{clip}\!\big(\alpha(\delta_{\text{PID}} - \delta_{t-1}), -\Delta\delta_{\max}, \Delta\delta_{\max}\big).
\end{align}
Longitudinal ACC velocity synchronization ($d_{\text{lead}} < 38$\,m, $v_{\text{lead}} \ge 8$\,km/h) \cite{ioannou1993autonomous,rajamani2001experimental,treiber2000congested} follows:
\begin{align}
    v_{\text{raw}} &= v_{\text{lead}} + 0.85(d_{\text{lead}} - 16.0), \\
    v_{\text{ACC}} &= \text{clip}\!\big(v_{\text{raw}},\, 0,\, \min(35.0, v_{\text{lead}} + 6.0)\big).
\end{align}
\begin{scriptsize}
\begin{verbatim}
# src/control/shield_pid.py (apply_safety_shield)
desired_gap_m = 16.0
gap_error_m = lead_distance_m - desired_gap_m
v_cap = max(target_speed_kmh, min(35.0, lead_speed_kmh + 6.0))
acc_sync_speed = float(np.clip(
    lead_speed_kmh + 0.85 * gap_error_m, 0.0, v_cap))
\end{verbatim}
\end{scriptsize}

\section{Experiments and Conclusion}
\label{sec:exp}
Closed-loop benchmarks were executed in MetaDrive 0.4.3 \cite{li2022metadrive} ($4,153$ frames) and live CARLA 0.9.16 \cite{dosovitskiy2017carla} (\texttt{Town10HD\_Opt}, $20$\,Hz synchronous mode with A* \texttt{GlobalRoutePlanner} \cite{hart1968formal}, $889$ frames in \texttt{driving\_demo\_carla\_smooth.mp4}).
