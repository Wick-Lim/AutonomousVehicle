\begin{abstract}
Autoregressive vision-language-action (VLA) models \cite{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla,liu2023visual} decode tokens sequentially ($>300$\,ms/frame), exceeding the $50$\,ms budget of $20$\,Hz vehicle control \cite{dosovitskiy2017carla,li2022metadrive}. We present a real-time hybrid closed-loop driving stack that couples frozen DINOv2 (\texttt{facebook/dinov2-base}, $86.58$M) \cite{oquab2024dinov2,caron2021emerging,dosovitskiy2021image} and frozen Laya (\texttt{convaiinnovations/laya}, $395.21$M transformer) \cite{laya2024nonautoregressive,warner2024modernbert,devlin2019bert} via a trainable $4 \times 9$ spatial-temporal adapter ($4.23$M parameters: $1.85$M spatial MLP, $1.05$M temporal gate, $1.05$M auxiliary perception head with decoupled lead/pedestrian depth and range-rate branches, and $0.28$M readout/trajectory heads) with HD-map routing, a rule-based safety shield, and kinematic path tracking. One synchronized forward+control pass takes $32.85$\,ms ($30.4$\,FPS on Ryzen~5 7500F / RTX~4070~Ti, FP32 $B=1$: $0.42$\,ms preprocess + $16.43$\,ms DINOv2 + $0.82$\,ms projector + $0.49$\,ms aux head + $13.55$\,ms Laya + $0.69$\,ms 6-WP trajectory head + $0.45$\,ms shield/PID; $31.67$\,ms base without aux/trajectory heads, excluding $6.40$\,ms simulator \texttt{world.tick()} IPC). Rather than performing free-space obstacle-avoidance path planning, the 6-waypoint trajectory head acts as a maneuver-conditioned curvature-smoothing regularizer supervised by parametric kinematic reference curves (open-loop trajectory collision rate $\text{CR}_{\text{traj}}=3.8\%$ before longitudinal CTH-ACC/AEB shielding vs.\ $0.0\%$ executed). In \texttt{vision\_estimated} mode, lead-vehicle speed $\hat{v}_{\text{lead}}$ is estimated via a 5-step ($0.25$\,s) least-squares regression slope fused with direct visual range-rate regression (reducing velocity jitter RMS from $9.62$ to $1.84$\,km/h, $\text{MAE}=2.41$\,km/h), while a dedicated pedestrian depth head resolves vehicle--pedestrian co-presence ambiguity ($\text{MAE}=2.64$\,m vs.\ $14.8$\,m coupled). On unseen closed-loop \texttt{vision\_estimated} episodes, teacher-rule $\text{ECE}_{\text{mean}}=0.0142$, independent physical-hazard $\text{ECE}_{\text{phys}}=0.0318$ (high-confidence error rate $\text{HCER}_{0.90}=1.12\%$, AEB $\text{HCER}_{0.90}=0.45\%$), and physical hazard AEB false-negative rate is $\text{FNR}_{\text{AEB}}=1/48$ events ($2.1\%$). Downstream control combines a 14-waypoint ($28$\,m lookahead, $2$\,m piecewise-linear spacing) spatial quintic transition $s(\tau) = 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5$ \cite{flash1985coordination,werling2010optimal}, chord-angle tracking \cite{thrun2006stanley,hoffmann2007autonomous,coulter1992implementation,paden2016survey}, fused map/trajectory bicycle curvature feedforward, a $1.5$\,s forward-lateral blind-spot latch (partial mitigation for previously seen flank vehicles), and constant-time-headway ACC \cite{ioannou1993autonomous,rajamani2001experimental,treiber2000congested}. Across CARLA 0.9.16 (\texttt{Town10HD\_Opt} $680$\,m paired, $3,400$\,m cross-weather, $2,000$\,m multi-block, plus \texttt{Town03} $2,000$\,m) and 50 unseen MetaDrive maps ($20,765$ steps, $9,520$\,m), \texttt{vision\_estimated} DINOv2+Laya achieves $47/50$ ($94.0\%$, 3-seed $46.7\pm 0.6$), showing an observed 5-episode margin over a 2-layer trainable Transformer ($42/50$, two-sided McNemar $p=0.063$, one-sided $p=0.031$) and significant gains ($p < 0.01$) over a 2-layer MLP ($39/50$), direct \texttt{ExpertLabeler} rules ($38/50$), random frozen Laya ($36/50$), prompt-only ($33/50$), and $300$\,ms lag ($34/50$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Problem Formulation}
Autoregressive decoders evaluate $P(y_{1:K} \mid I_t, \mathcal{T}_t) = \prod_{k=1}^{K} P(y_k \mid y_{<k}, I_t, \mathcal{T}_t)$ over $K \approx 15$ serial steps \cite{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla,liu2023visual}, whereas planning-oriented and sensor-fusion stacks \cite{hu2023planning,jiang2023vad,li2022bevformer,codevilla2018end,chen2020learning,prakash2021multi,chitta2022transfuser,jaeger2023hidden} require $\Delta t \le 50$\,ms. In our formulation, control at step $t$ reads three discrete tactical decisions and $K_w=6$ maneuver-conditioned kinematic reference waypoints ($t_k = 0.5k$\,s) in one bidirectional pass \cite{devlin2019bert,warner2024modernbert,laya2024nonautoregressive}:
\begin{align}
    m_t &\in \{\texttt{keep\_lane}, \texttt{change\_left}, \nonumber\\
        &\quad\; \texttt{change\_right}, \texttt{follow\_lead}\}, \\
    s_t &\in \{0, 1, 2, 3\} \mapsto \{0, 20, 35, 50\}\,\text{km/h}, \\
    p_{\text{AEB}} &\in [0, 1], \quad
    \hat{\tau}_t = \{(\hat{x}_k, \hat{y}_k, \hat{v}_k)\}_{k=1}^{6}.
\end{align}

\section{Architecture, Multi-Task Training, and Calibration}
\label{sec:arch}
Camera frames are cropped ($\texttt{crop\_top}=0.25$, $\texttt{crop\_bottom}=0.10$) and resized to $196 \times 392$. Frozen DINOv2 ViT-B/14 ($p=14$, $86.58$M params) \cite{oquab2024dinov2,caron2021emerging,dosovitskiy2021image} extracts $F_{\text{patch}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 392 \times 768}$ ($14 \times 28$ grid). \texttt{SpatialProjector} (\texttt{src/models/spatial\_projector.py}) pools $14 \times 28 \to 4 \times 9$ (36 tokens), adds $E_{\text{row}} + E_{\text{col}}$, projects $768 \to 1024$ via a 2-layer GELU MLP ($1.85$M core params), and fuses step-normalized temporal residual $\Delta V_t = (V_t - V_{t-\Delta n_t})/\Delta n_t$ ($1.05$M params, where $\Delta n_t \ge 1$ is elapsed frames so dropped frames scale proportionally) with region-pooled \texttt{AuxiliaryPerceptionHead} ($1.05$M params with decoupled heads for lead distance $\hat{d}_{\text{lead}}$, dedicated pedestrian distance $\hat{d}_{\text{ped}}$, visual range-rate $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis}}$, lane offset $\hat{e}_y$, traffic light $\hat{c}_{\text{TL}}$, hazard $\hat{c}_{\text{haz}}$, and forward-lateral blind spot $\hat{p}_{\text{BS}}$):
\begin{align}
    G_t &= \text{AdaptiveAvgPool2d}_{(4,9)}(\tilde{F}_t) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 768 \times 4 \times 9}, \\
    V_t &= \text{MLP}\big(\text{Flatten}(G_t + E_{\text{row}} + E_{\text{col}})\big), \\
    \tilde{V}_t &= V_t + \tanh(\alpha_{\text{temp}})\, W_{\text{temp}}\!\left(\frac{V_t - V_{t-\Delta n_t}}{\Delta n_t}\right).
\end{align}
Because $\Delta V_t$ is computed in token space without homography ego-motion compensation, ego yaw rotation on curves mixes background flow with object motion; however, $E_{\text{telemetry}}$ supplies ego yaw-rate context to Laya, and ablating $\Delta V_t$ (\texttt{no\_temporal}) reduces curved-road dynamic following (\texttt{FSD-10}) from $5/5 \to 3/5$. Multimodal sequence $Z = [e_{\text{[CLS]}}; \tilde{V}_t; E_{\text{telemetry}}; e_{\text{choice}}; e_{\text{score}}; e_{\text{noul}}]$ passes through frozen Laya ($395.21$M params) \cite{laya2024nonautoregressive,warner2024modernbert}. Linear heads ($W_m, W_s, W_b$) and 2-layer trajectory MLP $W_{\text{traj}}$ ($0.28$M params combined) predict discrete logits and bounded residuals $\Delta\hat{\tau} \in \mathbb{R}^{6 \times 3}$ over prior $\tau^0_k$ ($t_k = 0.5k$\,s), supervised against rule-generated parametric curve $\tau^{\text{ref}}_k = \big((v^*/3.6)t_k,\, y_{\text{shift}}(m^*)s(k/6),\, v^*\big)$:
\begin{align}
    \hat{\tau}_{1:6} &= \tau^0_{1:6} + \text{diag}(1.5, 0.35, 3.0)\, W_{\text{traj}}\bar{H}, \\
    \mathcal{L} &= w_m \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}^{(m)} + w_s \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}^{(s)} + w_a \mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}^{(b)} \nonumber\\
                &\quad + w_\tau \mathcal{L}_{\text{SmoothL1}}(\hat{\tau}_{1:6}, \tau^{\text{ref}}_{1:6}) + w_p \mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}}.
\end{align}
Because $\tau^{\text{ref}}_{1:6}$ is a parametric curve determined by $(m^*, v^*)$ rather than free-space obstacle optimization, $W_{\text{traj}}$ serves as a maneuver-conditioned curvature-smoothing regularizer rather than an independent collision-avoidance planner. In a counterfactual lateral obstacle-shift test ($\Delta y_{\text{obs}} \in \{-0.8, 0.0, +0.8\}$\,m at $d=22$\,m across $200$ frames), predicted $\hat{y}_6$ shifts by only $|\Delta\hat{y}_6| = 0.12$\,m within-lane when $m_t=\texttt{keep\_lane}$, versus $3.41$\,m when $m_t$ switches to $\texttt{change\_left/right}$. Evaluating the raw 6-WP trajectory open-loop over $3.0$\,s against ground-truth actor boxes yields a $3.8\%$ ($75/1,981$ frames) trajectory overlap rate ($\text{CR}_{\text{traj}}$) prior to longitudinal CTH-ACC/AEB shielding, versus $0.0\%$ executed collisions.

In Stage~1, all $4,232,991$ trainable parameters are jointly optimized for 15 epochs across 3 seeds ($42, 43, 44$; batch 32, AdamW \cite{loshchilov2019decoupled} $\text{lr}=3\times 10^{-4}$, $(w_m,w_s,w_a,w_\tau,w_p)=(1.0,1.0,2.0,0.5,0.2)$) on $50,000$ frames ($500$ episodes) split by contiguous episode blocks (\texttt{split\_by\_episode=True}: episodes $0..424$ train / $425..499$ val). Stage~2 (\texttt{03\_calibrate.py}) fits $(T_m, T_s, T_b) = (0.050, 0.050, 0.100)$ via L-BFGS \cite{guo2017calibration} ($M=15$ bins), reducing validation teacher-rule $\text{ECE}_{\text{mean}} = \frac{1}{3}(\text{ECE}_m + \text{ECE}_s + \text{ECE}_b)$ from $0.0060 \to 0.0000$. On unseen closed-loop \texttt{vision\_estimated} test episodes ($9,905$ frames across 50 episodes), teacher-rule alignment is $\text{ECE}_{\text{mean}}=0.0142$ ($\text{ECE}_b=0.0195$, $\text{NLL}=0.089$, $\text{Brier}=0.028$). Evaluated directly against independent physical hazard ground truth $y_{\text{phys},t} \in \{0,1\}$ ($\text{TTC} \le 1.8$\,s to any actor or $d \le 15$\,m to a red stop line, spanning $48$ physical hazard events / $220$ hazard ticks), physical-hazard calibration is $\text{ECE}_{\text{phys}}=0.0318$ ($\text{Brier}_{\text{phys}}=0.034$), high-confidence error rate at $\hat{p} \ge 0.90$ is $\text{HCER}_{0.90}=1.12\%$ across all 3 heads ($\text{HCER}_{0.90,\text{AEB}}=0.45\%$, $45/9,905$ frames), and event-level AEB false-negative rate is $\text{FNR}_{\text{AEB,evt}}=1/48$ ($2.1\%$, tick-level $6/220=2.7\%$).

\section{Input Provenance, Spatial Quintic Spline, Curvature Feedforward, and CTH-ACC}
\label{sec:control}
Table~\ref{tab:params} specifies the runtime provenance of every variable (\texttt{scripts/04\_evaluate\_sim.py}). In \texttt{vision\_estimated} mode, zero simulator actor queries are used. Because 1-step ($\Delta t=0.05$\,s) depth differencing amplifies $1$\,m depth noise into $72$\,km/h raw velocity spikes ($9.62$\,km/h RMS jitter even after $\beta=0.35$ EMA), we estimate range rate over a $K_w=5$-step ($W=0.25$\,s) sliding window via ordinary least-squares (OLS) linear regression $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{reg},t} = \frac{\sum_{j=-2}^{2} j\,\hat{d}_{\text{lead},t-2+j}}{10\Delta t}$ (reducing uncorrelated noise variance by $10\times$), fused with direct visual range-rate readout $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis},t}$ from \texttt{AuxiliaryPerceptionHead}: $\hat{\dot{d}}_t = 0.70\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{reg},t} + 0.30\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis},t}$, with automatic window reset when $|\hat{d}_{\text{lead},t} - \hat{d}_{\text{lead},t-1}| > 6.0$\,m on cut-in or target switch, followed by EMA ($\beta=0.35$) to yield $\hat{v}_{\text{lead},t} = \text{clip}((1-\beta)\hat{v}_{\text{lead},t-1} + \beta(v_{\text{ego},t} + 3.6\hat{\dot{d}}_t), 0, 60)$. Across closed-loop logs, this reduces frame-to-frame velocity jitter RMS from $9.62 \to 1.84$\,km/h ($\hat{v}_{\text{lead}}$ MAE $6.85 \to 2.41$\,km/h), recovers from $>6$\,m cut-in target switches in $0.15$\,s ($3$ frames), and detects lead hard braking (\texttt{FSD-6}) with $0.10$\,s ($2$-frame) lag. To prevent co-presence coupling when a lead vehicle (e.g., at $35$\,m) and a crosswalk pedestrian (e.g., at $12$\,m) appear simultaneously, \texttt{AuxiliaryPerceptionHead} includes a dedicated pedestrian distance branch $\hat{d}_{\text{ped},t}$ (\texttt{ped\_dist\_head}), reducing vehicle+pedestrian co-presence depth MAE from $14.8 \to 2.64$\,m. Because a single front camera ($90^\circ$ FOV) observes forward-lateral adjacent lanes ($x \in [0, 12]$\,m) and cannot see the rear flank ($x \in [-10, 0)$\,m), the 30-frame ($1.5$\,s) latch is a partial mitigation for previously seen vehicles ($3/3$ in \texttt{FSD-7}), whereas unobserved rear-approaching vehicles ($0/2$ in \texttt{vision\_estimated} vs.\ $1/2$ in \texttt{gt} BSM) require rear/corner sensors. When \texttt{--shield-mode on} is active, Rule~1 forces $u_{\text{tb}} = -1.0$ if $\hat{p}_{\text{AEB}} \ge 0.70$, $\text{TL}=\texttt{red}$, $d_{\text{ped}} \le 16.0$\,m, or $d_{\text{lead}} \le 11.5$\,m; Rule~2 blocks unsafe lane changes if $\max_c \hat{p}^{(m)}_c < 0.80$, $\texttt{adj\_wp.lane\_id} \times \texttt{wp.lane\_id} \le 0$ \cite{dupuis2010opendrive}, or $\text{BS}_{\text{L/R}}$ is active.

For lane transitions, $N_b = 14$ waypoints are sampled at $2.0$\,m spacing ahead of the ego axle ($d_0 = 0$\,m) at $d_k = 2.0(k+1)$\,m for $k \in \{0,\dots,13\}$ ($d_0=2.0$\,m to $d_{13}=28.0$\,m, spanning $26.0$\,m between $P_0$ and $P_{13}$ and $28.0$\,m total lookahead from the ego origin with $\tau_k = (k+1)/N_b$), blended via $s(\tau) = 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5$ \cite{flash1985coordination,werling2010optimal}:
\begin{align}
    s(\tau) &= 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5, \\
    P^{\text{blend}}_k &= \big(1 - s(\tau_k)\big) P^{\text{orig}}_k + s(\tau_k) P^{\text{adj}}_k.
\end{align}
While the continuous envelope satisfies $s'(0)=s'(1)=s''(0)=s''(1)=0$, runtime tracking projects onto the discrete $2.0$\,m piecewise-linear polyline $\overline{P^{\text{blend}}_k P^{\text{blend}}_{k+1}}$, which bounds node-to-node heading steps ($|\Delta\psi_k| \le 1.9^\circ$) rather than enforcing analytic $C^2$ continuity between nodes, with lookahead $L_{\text{look}} = \text{clip}(7.5 + 0.18 v_{\text{kmh}}, 7.5, 13.5)$\,m \cite{thrun2006stanley,hoffmann2007autonomous,coulter1992implementation,paden2016survey}. Map field \texttt{road\_curvature} stores right-handed heading change $\Delta\psi_{\text{arc}}$ over $L_{\text{arc}} = 22.2$\,m, so signed left-positive curvature is $\kappa_{\text{map}} = -\Delta\psi_{\text{arc}}/L_{\text{arc}} = -0.045\,\texttt{road\_curvature}$ ($\text{m}^{-1}$). Fusing $\kappa_{\text{map}}$ with waypoint $k=3$ ($t=1.5$\,s, $(\hat{x}_3, \hat{y}_3)$) curvature $\kappa_{\text{traj}}$ ($L=2.85$\,m, $\delta_{\max}=0.70$\,rad, $k_{\text{FF}}=0.12$) yields:
\begin{align}
    \kappa_{\text{traj}} &= \frac{2(\hat{y}_3 - e_y)}{\max(3.5, \hat{x}_3)^2 + (\hat{y}_3 - e_y)^2}, \\
    \kappa_{\text{eff}} &= 0.70(-0.045\,\texttt{road\_curvature}) + 0.30\kappa_{\text{traj}}, \\
    \delta_{\text{raw}} &= \text{clip}\!\left(\delta_{\text{PID}}(e_y, e_\psi) + k_{\text{FF}}\frac{\arctan(L\kappa_{\text{eff}})}{\delta_{\max}}, -1, 1\right), \\
    \Delta \delta_{\max} &= \frac{1.1\Delta t}{1 + 0.012 v_{\text{kmh}}}, \\
    \delta_t &= \delta_{t-1} + \text{clip}\!\big(\alpha(\delta_{\text{raw}} - \delta_{t-1}), -\Delta\delta_{\max}, \Delta\delta_{\max}\big).
\end{align}
True constant-time-headway (CTH) ACC ($d_{\text{lead}} < 38$\,m, $v_{\text{lead}} \ge 8$\,km/h, $d_0 = 8.0$\,m, $\tau_h = 1.2$\,s, $k_g = 0.85$\,(km/h)/m $\equiv 0.236\,\text{s}^{-1}$) \cite{ioannou1993autonomous,rajamani2001experimental,treiber2000congested} follows:
\begin{align}
    d^*(v_{\text{ego,kmh}}) &= d_0 + \tau_h \left(\frac{v_{\text{ego,kmh}}}{3.6}\right), \\
    v_{\text{raw}} &= v_{\text{lead}} + k_g \big(d_{\text{lead}} - d^*(v_{\text{ego,kmh}})\big), \\
    v_{\text{ACC}} &= \text{clip}\!\big(v_{\text{raw}},\, 0.0,\, \min(35.0,\, v_{\text{lead}} + 6.0)\big).
\end{align}
\begin{scriptsize}
\begin{verbatim}
# src/control/shield_pid.py
d_star = 8.0 + 1.2 * (current_speed_kmh / 3.6)
acc_cap = min(35.0, lead_speed_kmh + 6.0)
raw_acc = lead_speed_kmh + 0.85 * (lead_distance_m - d_star)
acc_sync_speed = float(np.clip(raw_acc, 0.0, acc_cap))
traj_curv = (2.0 * ly) / (lx * lx + ly * ly)

\end{verbatim}
\end{scriptsize}

\section{Experiments, Latency Breakdown, and Limitations}
\label{sec:exp}
Closed-loop benchmarks were executed on an AMD Ryzen~5 7500F (6C/12T) and NVIDIA GeForce RTX~4070~Ti (12\,GB) workstation in MetaDrive 0.4.3 \cite{li2022metadrive} ($\Delta t = 0.05$\,s control step via \texttt{decision\_repeat=5}; $415.3$ steps / $190.4$\,m per episode at $\bar{v}=33.0$\,km/h, totaling $20,765$ steps / $9,520$\,m across $N=50$ unseen maps) and live CARLA 0.9.16 \cite{dosovitskiy2017carla} (\texttt{Town10HD\_Opt} and unseen \texttt{Town03}, $20$\,Hz synchronous A* \texttt{GlobalRoutePlanner} \cite{hart1968formal}). Between MetaDrive and CARLA, the $196\times 392$ crop/resize pipeline, frozen DINOv2+Laya weights, \texttt{projector\_best.pt}, temperatures $(0.05,0.05,0.10)$, CTH-ACC law, and 14-WP quintic spline are kept identical without retraining, whereas vertical FOV ($90^\circ$ CARLA vs.\ $65^\circ$ MetaDrive) and map waypoint APIs are adapted per simulator. Per-stage latency measured across the same $1,981$ frames with per-stage \texttt{torch.cuda.synchronize()} (\texttt{checkpoints/eval\_metrics\_carla.json}):

All seven stages sum to $32.85$\,ms ($32.40$\,ms neural + $0.45$\,ms control), and omitting \texttt{auxiliary\_perception\_head} ($0.49$\,ms) and \texttt{trajectory\_head\_6wp} ($0.69$\,ms) yields $31.67$\,ms base latency ($39.25$\,ms total loop with $6.40$\,ms CARLA IPC $< 50$\,ms budget). In Table~\ref{tab:ablation}, an AEB event is defined reproducibly as $\ge 3$ contiguous ticks ($\ge 0.15$\,s) with $u_{\text{tb}} \le -0.50$ (bridging $\le 2$-tick gaps); isolated $1$--$2$ tick transients are counted in the tick total but equal $0$ sustained events (hence `5/0 (44/2)` in Full has $2$ isolated FP ticks and $0$ FP events, and $44$ TP ticks comprise $39$ model-initiated $\hat{p}_{\text{AEB}}\ge 0.70$ ticks plus $5$ shield lead/ped distance triggers). Paired two-sided McNemar tests show significant gains ($p < 0.01$) for DINOv2+Laya ($47/50$) over \texttt{mlp\_head} ($39/50, p=0.0078$), \texttt{expert\_rule\_policy} ($38/50, p=0.0039$), \texttt{random\_laya} ($36/50, p=0.0010$), and \texttt{prompt\_only} ($33/50, p=0.0001$), while the 5-episode margin over the 2-layer trainable Transformer ($42/50$, $p=0.0625$ two-sided, $p=0.0313$ one-sided) reflects an observed advantage on night/rain hazards. Removing $\Delta\hat{\tau}$ ($45/50$), $\kappa_{\text{traj}}$ ($44/50$), or $\kappa_{\text{map}}$ ($41/50$) confirms that closed-loop reliability arises from the hybrid synergy of visual inference, HD-map priors, and CTH-ACC/quintic control.
