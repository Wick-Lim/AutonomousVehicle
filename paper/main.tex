\begin{abstract}
Token-by-token vision-language-action (VLA) decoders \cite{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla,liu2023visual} take $>300$\,ms/frame, exceeding the $50$\,ms budget of $20$\,Hz control \cite{dosovitskiy2017carla,li2022metadrive,geiger2012we,caesar2020nuscenes,sun2020scalability}. Here we connect frozen DINOv2 (\texttt{facebook/dinov2-base}, $86.58$M) \cite{oquab2024dinov2,caron2021emerging,dosovitskiy2021image} and frozen Laya (\texttt{convaiinnovations/laya}, $395.21$M) \cite{laya2024nonautoregressive,warner2024modernbert,devlin2019bert} via a $4 \times 9$ adapter ($4.23$M weights: $1.85$M MLP, $1.05$M $\Delta n_t$-normalized temporal gate, $1.05$M aux head with decoupled $\hat{d}_{\text{lead}}$, $\hat{d}_{\text{ped}}$, and $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis}}$ branches, $0.28$M readout/6-WP heads) to an HD-map shield and PID tracker \cite{bojarski2016end,bansal2019chauffeurnet}. One synchronized pass takes $32.85$\,ms ($30.4$\,FPS on Ryzen~5 7500F / RTX~4070~Ti, FP32 $B=1$: $0.42 + 16.43 + 0.82 + 0.49 + 13.55 + 0.69 + 0.45 = 32.85$\,ms; $31.67$\,ms base without aux/traj; $+6.40$\,ms CARLA \texttt{world.tick()} IPC). Supervised by rule curves $\tau^{\text{ref}}_{1:6}$, the 6-WP head smooths curvature rather than planning obstacle detours (open-loop box overlap $\text{CR}_{\text{traj}}=3.8\%$ vs.\ $0.0\%$ closed-loop crashes). Under \texttt{vision\_estimated} mode, a 5-step ($0.25$\,s) ordinary least-squares (OLS) slope \cite{savitzky1964smoothing,kalman1960new} plus $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis}}$ cuts $\hat{v}_{\text{lead}}$ jitter RMS from $9.62 \to 1.84$\,km/h ($\text{MAE}=2.41$\,km/h), and dedicated \texttt{ped\_dist\_head} cuts vehicle+pedestrian co-presence MAE from $14.8 \to 2.64$\,m. On $9,905$ unseen frames, teacher $\text{ECE}_{\text{mean}}=0.0142$ \cite{guo2017calibration,zadrozny2002transforming,brier1950verification}, physical-hazard $\text{ECE}_{\text{phys}}=0.0318$ ($\text{HCER}_{0.90}=1.12\%$ all, $0.45\%$ AEB), and $\text{FNR}_{\text{AEB}}=1/48$ events ($2.1\%$). Control uses a 14-node ($d_k = 2(k+1)$\,m, $28$\,m lookahead) piecewise-linear quintic blend $s(\tau) = 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5$ \cite{flash1985coordination,werling2010optimal}, chord tracking \cite{thrun2006stanley,hoffmann2007autonomous,coulter1992implementation,paden2016survey}, bicycle curvature feedforward, a $1.5$\,s front-lateral latch, and CTH-ACC ($d^*(v_{\text{ego}}) = 8.0 + 1.2(v_{\text{ego}}/3.6)$, $k_g = 0.85$\,(km/h)/m) \cite{ioannou1993autonomous,rajamani2001experimental,treiber2000congested}. Across CARLA 0.9.16 and 50 unseen MetaDrive maps ($20,765$ steps, $9,520$\,m), \texttt{vision\_estimated} DINOv2+Laya finishes $47/50$ ($94.0\%$, $46.7\pm 0.6$) vs.\ $42/50$ for a 2-layer Transformer (McNemar \cite{mcnemar1947note} $p=0.063$ two-sided, $p=0.031$ one-sided) and $p < 0.01$ over 2-layer MLP ($39/50$), \texttt{ExpertLabeler} ($38/50$), random Laya ($36/50$), prompt-only ($33/50$), and $300$\,ms lag ($34/50$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Problem Formulation}
Evaluating $P(y_{1:K} \mid I_t, \mathcal{T}_t) = \prod_{k=1}^{K} P(y_k \mid y_{<k}, I_t, \mathcal{T}_t)$ over $K \approx 15$ serial steps \cite{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla,liu2023visual} violates $\Delta t \le 50$\,ms in $20$\,Hz planners \cite{hu2023planning,jiang2023vad,li2022bevformer,codevilla2018end,chen2020learning,prakash2021multi,chitta2022transfuser,jaeger2023hidden}. One bidirectional pass \cite{devlin2019bert,warner2024modernbert,laya2024nonautoregressive} at tick $t$ reads three discrete targets and $K_w=6$ reference points ($t_k = 0.5k$\,s):
\begin{align}
    m_t &\in \{\texttt{keep\_lane}, \texttt{change\_left}, \nonumber\\
        &\quad\; \texttt{change\_right}, \texttt{follow\_lead}\}, \\
    s_t &\in \{0, 1, 2, 3\} \mapsto \{0, 20, 35, 50\}\,\text{km/h}, \\
    p_{\text{AEB}} &\in [0, 1], \quad
    \hat{\tau}_t = \{(\hat{x}_k, \hat{y}_k, \hat{v}_k)\}_{k=1}^{6}.
\end{align}

\section{Architecture, Multi-Task Training, and Calibration}
\label{sec:arch}
Cropped RGB ($\texttt{crop\_top}=0.25$, $\texttt{crop\_bottom}=0.10$, $196 \times 392$) enters frozen DINOv2 ViT-B/14 ($p=14$, $86.58$M) \cite{oquab2024dinov2,caron2021emerging,dosovitskiy2021image} for $F_{\text{patch}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 392 \times 768}$ ($14 \times 28$). \texttt{SpatialProjector} (\texttt{src/models/spatial\_projector.py}) pools $(4, 9)$ (36 tokens), adds $E_{\text{row}} + E_{\text{col}}$, projects $768 \to 1024$ ($1.85$M MLP), and adds step-normalized residual $\Delta V_t = (V_t - V_{t-\Delta n_t})/\Delta n_t$ ($1.05$M params, $\Delta n_t \ge 1$ elapsed steps for frame drops; unwarped in token space so curve yaw mixes background flow with object motion, though $E_{\text{telemetry}}$ supplies ego yaw rate and \texttt{no\_temporal} drops wet-curve \texttt{FSD-10} from $5/5 \to 3/5$):
\begin{align}
    G_t &= \text{AdaptiveAvgPool2d}_{(4,9)}(\tilde{F}_t) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 768 \times 4 \times 9}, \\
    V_t &= \text{MLP}\big(\text{Flatten}(G_t + E_{\text{row}} + E_{\text{col}})\big), \\
    \tilde{V}_t &= V_t + \tanh(\alpha_{\text{temp}})\, W_{\text{temp}}\!\left(\frac{V_t - V_{t-\Delta n_t}}{\Delta n_t}\right).
\end{align}
\texttt{AuxiliaryPerceptionHead} ($1.05$M) predicts $\hat{c}_{\text{TL}}$ (rows $0..1$), $\hat{d}_{\text{lead}}$, dedicated $\hat{d}_{\text{ped}}$ (\texttt{ped\_dist\_head}), visual range-rate $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis}}$ (\texttt{range\_rate\_head}), $\hat{e}_y$, $\hat{c}_{\text{haz}}$ (cols $3..5$), and $\hat{p}_{\text{BS}}$ (cols $0..2, 6..8$). Frozen Laya ($395.21$M) \cite{laya2024nonautoregressive,warner2024modernbert} and heads $(W_m, W_s, W_b, W_{\text{traj}})$ ($0.28$M) fit rule curve $\tau^{\text{ref}}_k = \big((v^*/3.6)t_k,\, y_{\text{shift}}(m^*)s(k/6),\, v^*\big)$:
\begin{align}
    \hat{\tau}_{1:6} &= \tau^0_{1:6} + \text{diag}(1.5, 0.35, 3.0)\, W_{\text{traj}}\bar{H}, \\
    \mathcal{L} &= w_m \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}^{(m)} + w_s \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}^{(s)} + w_a \mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}^{(b)} \nonumber\\
                &\quad + w_\tau \mathcal{L}_{\text{SmoothL1}}(\hat{\tau}_{1:6}, \tau^{\text{ref}}_{1:6}) + w_p \mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}}.
\end{align}
Because $\tau^{\text{ref}}_k$ is rule-generated from $(m^*, v^*)$, $W_{\text{traj}}$ only smooths curvature; obstacle bypass comes from $m_t$ + HD-map spline (counterfactual obstacle shift $\Delta y_{\text{obs}} \in \{-0.8, 0, +0.8\}$\,m at $d=22$\,m moves $\hat{y}_6$ by $|\Delta\hat{y}_6|=0.12$\,m when $m_t=\texttt{keep\_lane}$ vs.\ $3.41$\,m on lane change; open-loop 3\,s box overlap $\text{CR}_{\text{traj}}=3.8\%$ on $75/1,981$ frames vs.\ $0.0\%$ closed-loop).

Stage~1 trains $4,232,991$ weights for 15 epochs (seeds $42, 43, 44$; batch 32, AdamW \cite{loshchilov2019decoupled} $\text{lr}=3\times 10^{-4}$, $(w_m,w_s,w_a,w_\tau,w_p)=(1,1,2,0.5,0.2)$) on $50,000$ frames ($500$ episodes, \texttt{split\_by\_episode=True}: $0..424$ train / $425..499$ val). Stage~2 (\texttt{03\_calibrate.py}) fits $(T_m, T_s, T_b) = (0.050, 0.050, 0.100)$ by L-BFGS \cite{guo2017calibration,zadrozny2002transforming} ($M=15$ bins; val $\text{ECE}_{\text{mean}}: 0.0060 \to 0.0000$). On $9,905$ unseen \texttt{vision\_estimated} frames (50 episodes), teacher $\text{ECE}_{\text{mean}}=0.0142$ ($\text{ECE}_b=0.0195$, $\text{NLL}=0.089$, $\text{Brier}=0.028$ \cite{brier1950verification}); against physical hazards $y_{\text{phys},t} \in \{0,1\}$ ($\text{TTC} \le 1.8$\,s or red-light $d \le 15$\,m; $48$ events / $220$ ticks), $\text{ECE}_{\text{phys}}=0.0318$ ($\text{Brier}_{\text{phys}}=0.034$), $\text{HCER}_{0.90}=1.12\%$ all ($\text{HCER}_{0.90,\text{AEB}}=0.45\%$, $45/9,905$), and $\text{FNR}_{\text{AEB,evt}}=1/48$ ($2.1\%$, $6/220$ ticks $= 2.7\%$).

\section{Input Provenance, Spatial Quintic Spline, Curvature Feedforward, and CTH-ACC}
\label{sec:control}
Table~\ref{tab:params} lists all input sources (\texttt{scripts/04\_evaluate\_sim.py}). Under \texttt{vision\_estimated}, 1-step ($\Delta t=0.05$\,s) differencing turns $1$\,m depth noise into $72$\,km/h spikes ($9.62$\,km/h RMS jitter after $\beta=0.35$ EMA). We fit a $K_w=5$-step ($W=0.25$\,s) OLS slope $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{reg},t}$ \cite{savitzky1964smoothing,kalman1960new} ($10\times$ lower noise variance), fuse $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis},t}$, reset the window on target jumps $|\hat{d}_{\text{lead},t} - \hat{d}_{\text{lead},t-1}| > 6.0$\,m, and apply $\beta=0.35$ EMA (cutting jitter RMS $9.62 \to 1.84$\,km/h, $\text{MAE}: 6.85 \to 2.41$\,km/h, cut-in recovery $0.15$\,s / $3$ frames, hard-brake lag $0.10$\,s / $2$ frames in \texttt{FSD-6}), while \texttt{ped\_dist\_head} $\hat{d}_{\text{ped},t}$ decouples pedestrian range from lead vehicles (co-presence MAE $14.8 \to 2.64$\,m):
\begin{align}
    \hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{reg},t} &= \frac{\sum_{j=-2}^{2} j\,\hat{d}_{\text{lead},t-2+j}}{10\Delta t}, \; \hat{\dot{d}}_t = 0.70\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{reg},t} + 0.30\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis},t}, \\
    \hat{v}_{\text{lead},t} &= \text{clip}\big((1-\beta)\hat{v}_{\text{lead},t-1} + \beta(v_{\text{ego},t} + 3.6\hat{\dot{d}}_t), 0, 60\big).
\end{align}
Front FOV ($90^\circ$) covers $x \in [0, 12]$\,m, so the 30-frame ($1.5$\,s) latch holds previously seen flank cars ($3/3$ in \texttt{FSD-7}) but is blind to rear approach from $x_0 = -15$\,m ($0/2$ Vis vs.\ $1/2$ \texttt{gt} BSM). Rule~1 (\texttt{--shield-mode on}) sets $u_{\text{tb}} = -1.0$ if $\hat{p}_{\text{AEB}} \ge 0.70$, $\text{TL}=\texttt{red}$, $d_{\text{ped}} \le 16.0$\,m, or $d_{\text{lead}} \le 11.5$\,m; Rule~2 cancels lane changes if $\max_c \hat{p}^{(m)}_c < 0.80$, $\texttt{adj\_wp.lane\_id} \times \texttt{wp.lane\_id} \le 0$ \cite{dupuis2010opendrive}, or $\text{BS}_{\text{L/R}}$ is latched.

For lane transitions, $N_b = 14$ nodes are sampled $2.0$\,m apart ahead of the ego axle ($d_0 = 0$\,m) at $d_k = 2.0(k+1)$\,m for $k \in \{0,\dots,13\}$ ($d_0=2.0$\,m to $d_{13}=28.0$\,m; $26.0$\,m between $P_0$ and $P_{13}$, $28.0$\,m lookahead from origin, $\tau_k = (k+1)/N_b$) via $s(\tau) = 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5$ \cite{flash1985coordination,werling2010optimal}:
\begin{align}
    s(\tau) &= 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5, \\
    P^{\text{blend}}_k &= \big(1 - s(\tau_k)\big) P^{\text{orig}}_k + s(\tau_k) P^{\text{adj}}_k.
\end{align}
Envelope $s'(0)=s'(1)=s''(0)=s''(1)=0$, while pure pursuit projects onto the $2.0$\,m piecewise-linear polyline $\overline{P^{\text{blend}}_k P^{\text{blend}}_{k+1}}$, bounding discrete heading steps ($|\Delta\psi_k| \le 1.9^\circ$) rather than analytic $C^2$ continuity between nodes, with $L_{\text{look}} = \text{clip}(7.5 + 0.18 v_{\text{kmh}}, 7.5, 13.5)$\,m \cite{thrun2006stanley,hoffmann2007autonomous,coulter1992implementation,paden2016survey}. With $\kappa_{\text{map}} = -\Delta\psi_{\text{arc}}/L_{\text{arc}} = -0.045\,\texttt{road\_curvature}$ ($\text{m}^{-1}$, $L_{\text{arc}} = 22.2$\,m), node $k=3$ ($t=1.5$\,s, $(\hat{x}_3, \hat{y}_3)$), $L=2.85$\,m, $\delta_{\max}=0.70$\,rad, $k_{\text{FF}}=0.12$, and CTH-ACC ($d_{\text{lead}} < 38$\,m, $v_{\text{lead}} \ge 8$\,km/h, $d_0 = 8.0$\,m, $\tau_h = 1.2$\,s, $k_g = 0.85$\,(km/h)/m $\equiv 0.236\,\text{s}^{-1}$) \cite{ioannou1993autonomous,rajamani2001experimental,treiber2000congested}:
\begin{align}
    \kappa_{\text{traj}} &= \frac{2(\hat{y}_3 - e_y)}{\max(3.5, \hat{x}_3)^2 + (\hat{y}_3 - e_y)^2}, \\
    \kappa_{\text{eff}} &= 0.70(-0.045\,\texttt{road\_curvature}) + 0.30\kappa_{\text{traj}}, \\
    \delta_{\text{raw}} &= \text{clip}\!\left(\delta_{\text{PID}}(e_y, e_\psi) + k_{\text{FF}}\frac{\arctan(L\kappa_{\text{eff}})}{\delta_{\max}}, -1, 1\right), \\
    \delta_t &= \delta_{t-1} + \text{clip}\!\big(\alpha(\delta_{\text{raw}} - \delta_{t-1}), -\Delta\delta_{\max}, \Delta\delta_{\max}\big), \\
    d^*(v_{\text{ego}}) &= d_0 + \tau_h (v_{\text{ego}}/3.6), \; v_{\text{raw}} = v_{\text{lead}} + k_g(d_{\text{lead}} - d^*), \\
    v_{\text{ACC}} &= \text{clip}\big(v_{\text{raw}},\, 0.0,\, \min(35.0,\, v_{\text{lead}} + 6.0)\big).
\end{align}

\section{Experiments, Latency Breakdown, and Limitations}
\label{sec:exp}
Runs used an AMD Ryzen~5 7500F (6C/12T) and RTX~4070~Ti (12\,GB) in MetaDrive 0.4.3 \cite{li2022metadrive} ($\Delta t = 0.05$\,s, \texttt{decision\_repeat=5}; $415.3$ steps / $190.4$\,m per run at $\bar{v}=33.0$\,km/h, $20,765$ steps / $9,520$\,m over $N=50$ unseen maps) and CARLA 0.9.16 \cite{dosovitskiy2017carla} (\texttt{Town10HD\_Opt} and \texttt{Town03}, $20$\,Hz A* \texttt{GlobalRoutePlanner} \cite{hart1968formal}). All seven stages sum to $32.85$\,ms ($32.40$\,ms neural + $0.45$\,ms control), and dropping \texttt{auxiliary\_perception\_head} ($0.49$\,ms) + \texttt{trajectory\_head\_6wp} ($0.69$\,ms) gives $31.67$\,ms base ($39.25$\,ms loop with $6.40$\,ms CARLA IPC $< 50$\,ms).
