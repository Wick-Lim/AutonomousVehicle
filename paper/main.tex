\begin{abstract}
Autoregressive vision-language-action (VLA) decoders \cite{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla,liu2023visual} take $>300$\,ms/frame, exceeding the $50$\,ms budget of $20$\,Hz control \cite{dosovitskiy2017carla,li2022metadrive,geiger2012we,caesar2020nuscenes,sun2020scalability}. We connect frozen DINOv2 (\texttt{facebook/dinov2-base}, $86.58$M) \cite{oquab2024dinov2,caron2021emerging,dosovitskiy2021image} and frozen Laya (\texttt{convaiinnovations/laya}, $395.21$M) \cite{laya2024nonautoregressive,warner2024modernbert,devlin2019bert} via a $4 \times 9$ adapter ($4.23$M params: $1.85$M MLP, $1.05$M $\Delta n_t$-normalized temporal gate, $1.05$M aux head with $\hat{d}_{\text{lead}}$, $\hat{d}_{\text{ped}}$, and $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis}}$ branches, $0.28$M readout/6-WP heads) to an HD-map shield and PID tracker \cite{bojarski2016end,bansal2019chauffeurnet}. One pass takes $32.85$\,ms ($30.4$\,FPS on Ryzen~5 7500F / RTX~4070~Ti, FP32 $B=1, 1.92$\,GB VRAM: $0.42 + 16.43 + 0.82 + 0.49 + 13.55 + 0.69 + 0.45 = 32.85$\,ms; $31.67$\,ms base; sensor-to-actuator $\text{AoI}$ mean $39.25$\,ms, P99 $44.12$\,ms, max $48.60$\,ms, $0/1,981 > 50$\,ms; $10$k-step drop rate $0.12\%$, $19.98$\,Hz obs). Trained on rule curves $\tau^{\text{ref}}_{1:6}$, the 6-WP head acts as a curvature regularizer ($+0.24$\,m clearance, $J_{y,\text{RMS}}: 1.41 \to 1.21\,\text{m/s}^3$) rather than an obstacle planner ($\text{CR}_{\text{traj}}=3.8\%$ open-loop vs.\ $0.0\%$ closed-loop). Under \texttt{vision\_estimated} mode, a 5-sample ($0.20$\,s span) timestamp-aware OLS slope \cite{savitzky1964smoothing,kalman1960new} over $(t_i, \hat{d}_i)$ plus $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis}}$ cuts $\hat{v}_{\text{lead}}$ jitter RMS from $9.62 \to 1.84$\,km/h ($\text{MAE}=2.41$; $15\%$ drop $\text{MAE}=2.68$ vs.\ $5.94$\,km/h uniform), and \texttt{ped\_dist\_head} cuts co-presence MAE from $14.8 \to 2.64$\,m. Across $60$ physical hazards ($9,905$ frames), episode-bootstrap $\text{ECE}_{\text{phys}}=0.0318$ ($95\%$ CI $[0.0241, 0.0405]$) \cite{guo2017calibration,zadrozny2002transforming,brier1950verification}, $\text{HCFNR}_{0.90}=1.82\%$ ticks ($1/48$ events), brake latency is $0.05$\,s median ($0.10$\,s P95), $54/60$ ($90.0\%$) are model-detected, $5/60$ ($8.3\%$) shield-saved, and $1/60$ ($1.7\%$) unmitigated. Control pairs a 14-node ($28$\,m) piecewise-linear quintic blend $s(\tau) = 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5$ \cite{flash1985coordination,werling2010optimal,thrun2006stanley,hoffmann2007autonomous,coulter1992implementation,paden2016survey} with stop-and-go CTH-ACC down to $0$\,km/h and closing-rate AEB $d_{\text{AEB}}(v_{\text{ego}},v_{\text{lead}})$ ($d_{\text{AEB}} < d^*$ for $\Delta v \le 6$\,km/h up to $50$\,km/h and for $v_{\text{ego}} < 41.8$\,km/h at $v_{\text{lead}}=0$, while intentionally preempting ACC above $41.8$\,km/h onto stopped targets) \cite{ioannou1993autonomous,rajamani2001experimental,treiber2000congested}. On $50$ MetaDrive maps ($100$\,Hz physics, \texttt{decision\_repeat=5} $\mapsto 20$\,Hz), \texttt{vision\_estimated} finishes $48/50$ ($96.0\%$ vs.\ $50/50$ \texttt{gt}). On CARLA 0.9.16 ($20$\,Hz sync), \texttt{vision\_estimated} finishes $47/50$ ($94.0\%$) and $93/100$ ($93.0\%$, $0.44$ crashes/km) vs.\ $83/100$ for a 2-layer Transformer (McNemar \cite{mcnemar1947note} $p=0.0063$, Holm--Bonferroni $p_{\text{Holm}}=0.0126$) and $p_{\text{Holm}} < 0.01$ over a matched 15-token AR head ($69/100$, $198.4$\,ms), 2-layer MLP ($77/100$), \texttt{ExpertLabeler} ($75/100$), and random $395$M Laya ($71/100$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction and Problem Formulation}
Decoding $P(y_{1:K} \mid I_t, \mathcal{T}_t) = \prod_{k=1}^{K} P(y_k \mid y_{<k}, I_t, \mathcal{T}_t)$ over $K \approx 15$ serial steps \cite{brohan2023rt2,kim2024openvla,liu2023visual} violates $\Delta t \le 50$\,ms in $20$\,Hz planners \cite{hu2023planning,jiang2023vad,li2022bevformer,codevilla2018end,chen2020learning,prakash2021multi,chitta2022transfuser,jaeger2023hidden}. One bidirectional pass \cite{devlin2019bert,warner2024modernbert,laya2024nonautoregressive} at tick $t$ outputs discrete decisions and $K_w=6$ reference points ($t_k = 0.5k$\,s):
\begin{align}
    m_t &\in \{\texttt{keep\_lane}, \texttt{change\_left}, \nonumber\\
        &\quad\; \texttt{change\_right}, \texttt{follow\_lead}\}, \\
    s_t &\in \{0, 1, 2, 3\} \mapsto \{0, 20, 40, 65\}\,\text{km/h}, \\
    p_{\text{AEB}} &\in [0, 1], \quad
    \hat{\tau}_t = \{(\hat{x}_k, \hat{y}_k, \hat{v}_k)\}_{k=1}^{6}.
\end{align}

\section{Architecture, Multi-Task Training, and Calibration}
\label{sec:arch}
Cropped RGB ($\texttt{crop\_top}=0.25$, $\texttt{crop\_bottom}=0.10$, $196 \times 392$) feeds frozen DINOv2 ViT-B/14 ($p=14$, $86.58$M) \cite{oquab2024dinov2,caron2021emerging,dosovitskiy2021image} for $F_{\text{patch}} \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 392 \times 768}$ ($14 \times 28$). \texttt{SpatialProjector} (\texttt{src/models/spatial\_projector.py}) pools $(4, 9)$ (36 tokens), adds $E_{\text{row}} + E_{\text{col}}$, projects $768 \to 1024$ ($1.85$M MLP), and adds step-normalized $\Delta V_t = (V_t - V_{t-\Delta n_t})/\Delta n_t$ ($1.05$M, $\Delta n_t \ge 1$ elapsed steps; unwarped in token space so curve yaw mixes background flow with object motion, though $E_{\text{telemetry}}$ supplies yaw rate and \texttt{no\_temporal} drops \texttt{FSD-10} from $5/5 \to 3/5$):
\begin{align}
    G_t &= \text{AdaptiveAvgPool2d}_{(4,9)}(\tilde{F}_t) \in \mathbb{R}^{B \times 768 \times 4 \times 9}, \\
    V_t &= \text{MLP}\big(\text{Flatten}(G_t + E_{\text{row}} + E_{\text{col}})\big), \\
    \tilde{V}_t &= V_t + \tanh(\alpha_{\text{temp}})\, W_{\text{temp}}\!\left(\frac{V_t - V_{t-\Delta n_t}}{\Delta n_t}\right).
\end{align}
\texttt{AuxiliaryPerceptionHead} ($1.05$M) predicts $\hat{c}_{\text{TL}}$ (rows $0..1$), $\hat{d}_{\text{lead}}$, dedicated $\hat{d}_{\text{ped}}$ (\texttt{ped\_dist\_head}), visual range-rate $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis}}$ (\texttt{range\_rate\_head}), $\hat{e}_y$, $\hat{c}_{\text{haz}}$ (cols $3..5$), and $\hat{p}_{\text{BS}}$ (cols $0..2, 6..8$). Frozen Laya ($395.21$M) \cite{laya2024nonautoregressive,warner2024modernbert} and heads $(W_m, W_s, W_b, W_{\text{traj}})$ ($0.28$M) fit rule curve $\tau^{\text{ref}}_k = \big((v^*/3.6)t_k,\, y_{\text{shift}}(m^*)s(k/6),\, v^*\big)$:
\begin{align}
    \hat{\tau}_{1:6} &= \tau^0_{1:6} + \text{diag}(1.5, 0.35, 3.0)\, W_{\text{traj}}\bar{H}, \\
    \mathcal{L} &= w_m \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}^{(m)} + w_s \mathcal{L}_{\text{CE}}^{(s)} + w_a \mathcal{L}_{\text{BCE}}^{(b)} \nonumber\\
                &\quad + w_\tau \mathcal{L}_{\text{SmoothL1}}(\hat{\tau}_{1:6}, \tau^{\text{ref}}_{1:6}) + w_p \mathcal{L}_{\text{aux}}.
\end{align}
Since $\tau^{\text{ref}}_k$ is rule-generated from $(m^*, v^*)$, $W_{\text{traj}}$ regularizes curvature ($+0.24$\,m clearance, $J_{y,\text{RMS}}: 1.41 \to 1.21\,\text{m/s}^3$, $\overline{|e_y|}: 0.105 \to 0.094$\,m on fixed map routes), while obstacle bypass uses $m_t$ + HD-map spline (varying $\Delta y_{\text{obs}} \in \{-0.8, 0, +0.8\}$\,m, width $1.8..2.4$\,m, and speed $0..5$\,m/s at $d=22$\,m shifts $|\Delta\hat{y}_6| \le 0.14$\,m under \texttt{keep\_lane} vs.\ $3.41$\,m on lane change; open-loop $\text{CR}_{\text{traj}}=3.8\%$ vs.\ $0.0\%$ closed-loop).
\begin{scriptsize}
\begin{verbatim}

Stage~1 trains $4,232,991$ weights for 15 epochs (seeds $42..44$; batch 32, AdamW \cite{loshchilov2019decoupled} $\text{lr}=3\times 10^{-4}$, $(w_m,w_s,w_a,w_\tau,w_p)=(1,1,2,0.5,0.2)$) on $50,000$ frames ($500$ eps, \texttt{split\_by\_episode=True}: $0..424$ train / $425..499$ teacher val). Stage~2 (\texttt{03\_calibrate.py}) fits $(T_m, T_s, T_b) = (0.050, 0.050, 0.100)$ on teacher val ($7,500$ frames; $\text{ECE}_{\text{mean}}: 0.0060 \to 0.0000$) \cite{guo2017calibration,zadrozny2002transforming}. On the disjoint unseen test ($50$ eps, $9,905$ frames, $60$ hazards: $48$ longitudinal $\text{TTC}\le 1.8$\,s / red-light $d\le 15$\,m + $12$ workzone/cut-in; $220$ ticks), teacher $\text{ECE}_{\text{mean}}=0.0142$ ($\text{Brier}=0.028$ \cite{brier1950verification}), $1,000$-sample episode-bootstrap $\text{ECE}_{\text{phys}}=0.0318$ ($95\%$ CI $[0.0241, 0.0405]$; ped $0.026$, red-light $0.019$, lead-stop $0.041$, workzone $0.038$), $\text{HCER}_{0.90,\text{AEB}}=0.45\%$, $\text{HCFNR}_{0.90}=4/220$ ticks ($1.82\%$, $1/48$ events), brake $\Delta t_{\text{brake}}$ is $0.05$\,s median ($0.10$\,s P95), $54/60$ ($90.0\%$, $[79.5, 96.2]\%$) are model-detected, $5/60$ ($8.3\%$, $[2.8, 18.4]\%$) shield-saved, and $1/60$ ($1.7\%$, $[0.04, 8.9]\%$) unmitigated.

\section{Input Provenance, Spatial Quintic Spline, Curvature Feedforward, and CTH-ACC}
\label{sec:control}
Table~\ref{tab:params} lists all input sources (\texttt{scripts/04\_evaluate\_sim.py}). Under \texttt{vision\_estimated}, 1-step ($\Delta t=0.05$\,s) differencing turns $1$\,m depth noise into $72$\,km/h spikes ($9.62$\,km/h RMS jitter). We fit a $K_w=5$-sample ($0.20$\,s span) timestamp-aware OLS slope $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{reg},t}$ \cite{savitzky1964smoothing,kalman1960new} over $(t_i, \hat{d}_i)$ with $t_i = t_{i-1} + \Delta n_{t_i}\Delta t$ ($15\%$ drop $\text{MAE}=2.68$ vs.\ $5.94$\,km/h uniform), fuse $\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis},t}$, reset on $|\Delta\hat{d}_{\text{lead},t}| > 6.0$\,m, and apply $\beta=0.35$ EMA (jitter RMS $9.62 \to 1.84$\,km/h, $\text{MAE}: 6.85 \to 2.41$\,km/h, cut-in recovery $0.15$\,s, brake lag $0.10$\,s), while \texttt{ped\_dist\_head} $\hat{d}_{\text{ped},t}$ cuts co-presence MAE ($14.8 \to 2.64$\,m):
\begin{align}
    \hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{reg},t} &= \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{K_w} (t_i - \bar{t})(\hat{d}_i - \bar{d})}{\sum_{i=1}^{K_w} (t_i - \bar{t})^2}, \; \hat{\dot{d}}_t = 0.7\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{reg},t} + 0.3\hat{\dot{d}}_{\text{vis},t}, \\
    \hat{v}_{\text{lead},t} &= \text{clip}\big((1-\beta)\hat{v}_{\text{lead},t-1} + \beta(v_{\text{ego},t} + 3.6\hat{\dot{d}}_t), 0, 60\big).
\end{align}
Front FOV ($90^\circ$, $x \in [0, 12]$\,m) + $1.5$\,s latch holds seen flank cars ($3/3$ \texttt{FSD-7}) but misses rear approach from $x_0 = -15$\,m ($0/2$ Vis vs.\ $1/2$ \texttt{gt}). Rule~1 (\texttt{--shield-mode on}) sets $u_{\text{tb}} = -1.0$ if $\hat{p}_{\text{AEB}} \ge 0.70$, $\text{TL}=\texttt{red}$, $d_{\text{ped}} \le 16.0$\,m, or $d_{\text{lead}} \le d_{\text{AEB}}(v_{\text{ego}}, v_{\text{lead}})$ with $v_{\text{ego}} > \max(6, v_{\text{lead}} + 6)$\,km/h ($a_{\max}=6.0\,\text{m/s}^2$ dry, $4.5\,\text{m/s}^2$ wet, margin $\delta_{\text{unc}}=1.8$\,m: $d_{\text{AEB}} < d^*$ for $\Delta v\le 6$\,km/h across $[0,50]$\,km/h and for $v_{\text{ego}} < 41.8$\,km/h at $v_{\text{lead}}=0$, while at $v_{\text{ego}} \ge 41.8$\,km/h onto a stopped target, e.g., $d_{\text{AEB}}(50,0)=27.83 > d^*(50)=24.67$\,m, Rule~1 intentionally preempts ACC); Rule~2 cancels lane changes if $\max_c \hat{p}^{(m)}_c < 0.80$, $\texttt{adj\_wp.lane\_id} \times \texttt{wp.lane\_id} \le 0$ \cite{dupuis2010opendrive}, or $\text{BS}_{\text{L/R}}$ is latched.

For lane changes, $N_b = 14$ nodes at $d_k = 2(k+1)$\,m ($k \in \{0..13\}$, $28$\,m lookahead, $\tau_k = (k+1)/N_b$) blend via $s(\tau) = 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5$ \cite{flash1985coordination,werling2010optimal}:
\begin{align}
    s(\tau) &= 10\tau^3 - 15\tau^4 + 6\tau^5, \\
    P^{\text{blend}}_k &= \big(1 - s(\tau_k)\big) P^{\text{orig}}_k + s(\tau_k) P^{\text{adj}}_k.
\end{align}
Pure pursuit projects onto the $2$\,m polyline $\overline{P^{\text{blend}}_k P^{\text{blend}}_{k+1}}$ ($|\Delta\psi_k| \le 1.9^\circ$, $L_{\text{look}} = \text{clip}(7.5 + 0.18 v_{\text{kmh}}, 7.5, 13.5)$\,m) \cite{thrun2006stanley,hoffmann2007autonomous,coulter1992implementation,paden2016survey}. With $\kappa_{\text{map}} = -0.045\,\texttt{road\_curvature}$ ($\text{m}^{-1}$, $L_{\text{arc}} = 22.2$\,m), node $k=3$ ($t=1.5$\,s), $L=2.85$\,m, $\delta_{\max}=0.70$\,rad, $k_{\text{FF}}=0.12$, and stop-and-go CTH-ACC down to $v_{\text{lead}} \ge 0$\,km/h ($d_{\text{lead}} < 38$\,m, $d_0 = 8.0$\,m, $\tau_h = 1.2$\,s, $k_g = 0.85$\,(km/h)/m, stop-hold at $v_{\text{lead}} < 2.0$\,km/h, $d_{\text{lead}} \le 8.5$\,m and resume at $v_{\text{lead}} \ge 3.5$\,km/h or $d_{\text{lead}} \ge 9.5$\,m: $0$ chattering, $0.20$\,s restart) \cite{ioannou1993autonomous,rajamani2001experimental,treiber2000congested}:
\begin{align}
    \kappa_{\text{traj}} &= \frac{2(\hat{y}_3 - e_y)}{\max(3.5, \hat{x}_3)^2 + (\hat{y}_3 - e_y)^2}, \\
    \kappa_{\text{eff}} &= 0.70(-0.045\,\texttt{road\_curvature}) + 0.30\kappa_{\text{traj}}, \\
    \delta_{\text{raw}} &= \text{clip}\!\left(\delta_{\text{PID}}(e_y, e_\psi) + k_{\text{FF}}\frac{\arctan(L\kappa_{\text{eff}})}{\delta_{\max}}, -1, 1\right), \\
    \delta_t &= \delta_{t-1} + \text{clip}\!\big(\alpha(\delta_{\text{raw}} - \delta_{t-1}), -\Delta\delta_{\max}, \Delta\delta_{\max}\big), \\
    d_{\text{AEB}} &= 5.5 + \frac{v_{\text{ego,s}}^2}{2a_{\max}} + 0.45\max(0, v_{\text{ego,s}} - v_{\text{lead,s}}) + \delta_{\text{unc}}, \\
    d^*(v_{\text{ego}}) &= d_0 + \tau_h (v_{\text{ego}}/3.6), \\
    v_{\text{raw}} &= v_{\text{lead}} + k_g(d_{\text{lead}} - d^*(v_{\text{ego}})), \\
    v_{\text{ACC}} &= \text{clip}\big(v_{\text{raw}}, 0, \min(35, v_{\text{lead}} + 6)\big).
\end{align}
All real-time CPU control, safety gating, timestamp-aware OLS, quintic spline blending, polyline projection, and \texttt{ExpertLabeler} primitives execute in a native \texttt{PyO3} Rust extension (\texttt{rust\_core/src/lib.rs}, \texttt{autonomous\_vehicle\_rust}), cutting per-step CPU control+OLS+geometry latency by $12.8\times$ from $113.48\,\mu\text{s}$ to $8.88\,\mu\text{s}$ ($0.336\,\mu\text{s}$ pure Rust core, $<10^{-6}$ numerical equivalence):
\begin{scriptsize}
\begin{verbatim}

\section{Experiments, Latency Breakdown, and Limitations}
\label{sec:exp}
Runs used a Ryzen~5 7500F and RTX~4070~Ti in MetaDrive 0.4.3 \cite{li2022metadrive} ($100$\,Hz physics, \texttt{decision\_repeat=5} $\mapsto 20$\,Hz / $50$\,ms policy loop; $9,520$\,m over $N=50$ maps: $48/50$ Vis vs.\ $50/50$ \texttt{gt}) and CARLA 0.9.16 \cite{dosovitskiy2017carla} ($20$\,Hz synchronous sensor, inference, and control ticks, $\Delta t=50$\,ms; A* \cite{hart1968formal}).
